# Medibank OSHC fact sheet for the agent

Use only these facts (or facts the user provides) in content. Anything else goes in
as `【待确认：…】`. **Medibank must review this file before each semester intake**,
because benefits, trials and offers change.

Source: medibank.com.au/overseas-student-health-cover (accessed 29 Oct 2026) and the
Department of Health and Aged Care OSHC fact sheet (Aug 2026).

## About OSHC (neutral, safe to explain)

| Fact | How to say it on 小红书 |
|------|-------------------------|
| OSHC is a condition of the Student visa (subclass 500), including partners and dependants on the visa. | 持学生签证（500）在澳洲读书，需要全程持有 OSHC（海外学生健康保险）。具体签证要求以澳洲内政部官网为准。 |
| OSHC must be bought before arriving and kept for the whole study period. | 一般需要在出发前买好，并覆盖整个学习期。 |
| Five OSHC insurers exist; students can choose their own and don't have to accept the school's pick. | Do **not** list or compare insurers by name. You may say "学生可以自己选择 OSHC 保险公司". |
| Basic OSHC covers GP and specialist visits (benefit of 85% of the MBS fee), public/private hospital treatment, surgery, emergency ambulance, and some prescription medicines; gap fees may apply. | Use as general explainer content. Always add "可能有自付差额（gap），以保单条款为准". |
| OSHC is not Medicare. | Good explainer topic: "OSHC 和 Medicare 有什么区别". |
