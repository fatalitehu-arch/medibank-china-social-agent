## 2. Scoring (out of 100)

Five dimensions, 1–5 each. Total × 4 = score.

| Dimension | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|
| Fit | IP属地 matches target region and identity is confirmed in bio or content | Location or identity unclear or hidden |
| Size | <2k followers (target band) | 2k+ followers |
| Activity | Posted within the last few days, regular cadence | Inactive or only a handful of posts |
| Authenticity | Real personal account, no ads, healthy follower/like ratios | Suspected matrix/marketing account, fake followers, heavy soft ads |
| Content | Everyday-life storytelling with its own voice and good topic range | Low text, low topic value, or off-target |
