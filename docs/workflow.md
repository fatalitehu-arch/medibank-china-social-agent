# Team workflow

## Roles

| Role | Who (suggested) | Uses the agent for |
|------|-----------------|--------------------|
| Social lead | Medibank international student marketing | Monthly plan (mode B), brand notes (mode A), final call on publishing |
| Creator manager | Same team or agency | Creator search, scoring, briefs, draft reviews (mode C) |
| Compliance reviewer | Medibank Legal & Compliance | Reviews every 🟡 item, every creator campaign, every high-reach note |
| Data owner | Digital team | Updates `brand-facts.md` each intake; tracks site and chatbot traffic |

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Task | Agent mode |
|-----|------|-----------|
| Mon | Check last week's numbers; pick 2–3 brand topics and 1–3 creator slots | B |
| Tue | Draft brand notes; send creator briefs through 蒲公英 | A, C |
| Wed–Thu | Review creator drafts; fix and re-check | C, D |
| Thu | Legal review of anything 🟡 or creator-made | (human) |
| Fri–Sun | Publish 19:00–22:00 Beijing time; reply to comments with the prepared replies | (human) |

## Before anything goes live

1. Agent verdict is 🟢, or every 🟡 item has been fixed or signed off.
2. Every fact matches `brand-facts.md` (no `【待确认】` left).
3. Creator notes are booked through 蒲公英 and will show the sponsor tag.
4. No links, QR codes, WeChat IDs or phone numbers in text or images.
5. Images use mock-ups, never a real student's offer letter or policy.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Note throttled (限流) or removed | Record the note and the likely trigger words; add them to `rules.json` with the date |
| Comment asks for price / to buy | Use the prepared replies in `kol-playbook.md` §7; never quote |
| Creator posts without approval | Ask them to edit or remove; log it for the 90-day review |
| Regulator or platform notice | Stop the campaign; escalate to Legal |

## Measuring success (monthly)

See `student-strategy.md` → "What to measure". The key number is visits to the
university Medibank OSHC site and chatbot sessions from China-based students during
offer and pre-departure windows.
