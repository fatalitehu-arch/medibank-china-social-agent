# Content playbook (all platforms)

## Content pillars

Every piece of content belongs to one pillar. Use these exact pillar names (the tools
check them). Target monthly mix across platforms:

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **看懂你的保险** (Know your cover) | 35% | offer letter 里 OSHC 那一栏怎么看; 保单日期要覆盖整个签证; 中介帮你办了，你也要知道这 5 件事 |
| **在澳洲看病** (Using healthcare) | 30% | 第一次在悉尼看 GP; GP、急诊、000 的区别; 半夜发烧怎么办 |
| **用好 app 和热线** (Use the services) | 20% | 3 步在 app 里报销; 24 小时热线能帮你什么（护士、心理、翻译、法律） |
| **身心健康** (Wellbeing) | 15% | 开学焦虑; 考试周睡不好; 冬天流感季准备 |

## Brand voice (same on every platform)

Persona: **一位在澳洲读过书的学姐**, working for Medibank, who has seen many new
students get stuck. Speaks plainly, warmly, with concrete detail.

* Talk to one person: 你, not 各位同学们. (For parents on 视频号/公众号: 您 is fine.)
* Start from a scene, not a definition: "落地第三天嗓子疼，打开地图发现附近的诊所都要预约…"
* Concrete beats general: name the step, the screen, the document.
* Honest about limits: if something has conditions, say them.
* No 广告腔: 赶快、立即、超值、限时、强烈推荐.
* Brand accounts say they are Medibank. They never pretend to be a random student.

| Platform | Shape of the same idea ("offer letter 里 OSHC 怎么看") |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| 小红书 | 图文: cover "拿到 offer 先看这一栏" + 6 images, one step each; 300–800 chars |
| 公众号 | Full guide, 1,200–2,500 chars with sub-heads, mock offer letter image, FAQ at the end; the version everyone else points to |
| 视频号 | 60 s: staff member or creator holds the mock letter and points at 3 fields; "完整版在公众号" |
| 抖音 | 30 s creator vlog: "我出发前才发现自己没看过保单" + 3 quick checks |
| 微博 | 120-char tip + 1 image + #澳洲留学# during intake weeks |

`skills/cn-repurpose` turns one approved piece into all five.

## Templates

### 图文 explainer (小红书, 抖音 图文, 微博 images)

```
[Hook: one concrete scene or question, 1–2 lines]
[Why it matters to you, 1–2 lines]
📄 第一步 …
📄 第二步 …
📄 第三步 …
[One honest caveat / 以官网条款为准]
[Soft next step in words: 去学校官网搜 OSHC]
[Interaction question: 你当时是自己办的还是中介办的？]
```

### 公众号 guide

```
标题（≤30 字） + 摘要（≤120 字）
开头：一个具体场景（2–3 段）
一、… 二、… 三、…（每节配图：模拟文件 / app 截图，去掉个人信息）
常见问题（3–5 个，答案来自 brand-facts.md）
下一步：学校官网 OSHC 页面；保险 app；24 小时热线（文字说明）
声明：本文为科普信息，具体保障以保单条款为准
```

### Short-video script (视频号, 抖音, 小红书视频)

```
0–3 s   Hook on screen + voice: "你知道你的 OSHC 什么时候到期吗？"
3–20 s  Show the 3 things to check (screen recording / mock offer letter)
20–40 s One real scene (creator) or service explainer (brand)
40–55 s Where to look next, in words
55–60 s Question for comments
```

Always: subtitles, no QR code or phone number on screen, mock documents only.
