# Creator collaboration terms checklist

What a Medibank creator agreement (or the platform marketplace order + side letter) should cover.
This is a checklist for Medibank Legal and Procurement, **not** a contract.

## Must have

| Term | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Booked and paid through the platform marketplace** (蒲公英 / 星图 / 互选 / 微任务), sponsor tag kept | PL-1; CN-5; AU-2 |
| **Flat fee per post or video.** No commission, bonus or referral fee tied to policies sold, quotes, or sign-ups | Paying per sale could make the creator an unlicensed insurance intermediary in China (CN-1, CN-2) |
| **Pre-publication review**: draft to Medibank, up to 2 rounds, 2 business days per round | Compliance gate |
| **Genuine experience**: creator only describes what they actually did or saw; Medibank won't script experiences | ACCC guidance (AU-2) |
| **Approved facts only**, with qualifiers, from the brief | ACL s 18/29 (AU-1) |
| **No diversion** (WeChat, QR, links, phone) and **no data collection** in note, comments or DMs | PL-3; CN-6 |
| **No selling or "helping buy"** in comments or DMs; use the prepared replies | CN-1/CN-2 |
| **Exclusivity**: no other OSHC insurer collaboration from 30 days before to 60 days after publishing | Credibility; avoids comparison content |
| **Takedown / edit right** if a factual error or compliance issue is found after publishing | Risk control |
| **Usage rights**: whether Medibank can repost the note on its own account, for how long | Content reuse |
| **Conduct clause**: Medibank may end the collaboration if the creator's content breaks law or platform rules | Brand safety |
| **Privacy**: no real student documents in images; blur any personal data | CN-6, AU-7 |

## Nice to have

* A 2-week comment-management window: creator answers common questions with the prepared
  replies and flags complaints to Medibank.
* A short debrief (screenshots of note data) 14 days after publishing.

## Red flags in a creator's counter-proposal

* "We'll post it as a personal share, no tag, cheaper" → reject (PL-1)
* "We usually take a % of sales" → reject (CN-1/CN-2)
* "We'll put our WeChat for questions" → reject (PL-3)
* "We need the full script from you" → offer facts + angle, not a script (AU-2)
