# Crisis and negative-feedback playbook (舆情)

## Where crises start

微博 is where complaints spread fastest and where media pick them up; 小红书 complaint
notes rank in search for months; WeChat issues spread in private group chats and surface
late. Monitor 微博 and 小红书 weekly (`references/platforms/weibo.md`).

## Levels

| Level | Signs | Owner | Response time |
|-------|-------|-------|---------------|
| **L1** | One complaint about a claim, waiting time, a service experience | Social team | Same day |
| **L2** | A complaint note gaining traction (100+ comments / reposts), a creator criticising Medibank, several similar complaints in a week | Social lead + Customer team | Within 4 hours: holding reply approved |
| **L3** | Media pick-up, regulator mention, data/privacy issue, health or safety incident, anything involving a death or serious harm, a cyber incident | Legal + Corporate Comms | Immediately: no public reply until approved |

## L1: one complaint

1. Reply once, publicly, with empathy and a path:
   > 看到你的经历，真的很抱歉让你这么折腾。为了查清楚具体情况，麻烦通过 Medibank 官网的客户服务渠道联系我们，我们会认真跟进。
2. Don't ask for personal details in comments. Don't debate facts. Don't offer
   compensation in public.
3. Log it (date, link, issue type) for the monthly report.

## L2: spreading complaint

1. Hold: stop scheduled promotional notes for 48 hours.
2. Draft a short holding reply for approval:
   > 我们注意到了大家的反馈，已经在核实具体情况。有相同经历的同学，可以通过 Medibank 官网的客户服务渠道联系我们，我们会逐一跟进。
3. Summarise for the social lead: what happened, how far it has spread, what people are
   asking, what we know and don't know.
4. If a creator is involved: talk to them privately through the platform marketplace; never pressure them to
   delete honest criticism.

## L3: serious

1. No public reply from the social team.
2. Prepare an internal brief: timeline, links/screenshots, reach, key claims, open
   questions.
3. Pause all scheduled content, including creator posts (tell creators through the marketplace).

## Mental-health content and comments

* If a comment suggests someone may harm themselves: reply quickly and gently, with
  emergency numbers, and alert the social lead.
  > 谢谢你愿意说出来。如果你现在有危险，请马上拨打 000。也可以联系 Lifeline 13 11 14（24 小时）。你的保险里也有 24 小时学生健康支持热线，可以找人聊聊。
* Never diagnose, never promise outcomes, never use the case in marketing.

## Never

* Delete critical comments that don't break rules (this escalates a crisis)
* Reply from personal accounts or ask friends to post defences
* Blame the student, the university or the education agent
* Mention competitors
