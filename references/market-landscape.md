# Market landscape (INTERNAL ONLY, never in public content)

> This file helps the agent plan and position. Competitors are **never** named or compared
> in notes, replies or creator briefs (CN-2).

## OSHC providers

The Department of Health and Aged Care OSHC fact sheet (Aug 2026) lists the OSHC insurers
as Allianz Care Australia, Bupa, Medibank and ahm (ahm is part of Medibank), and nib.
Students may choose any of them; universities often have a preferred provider (UNSW's is
Medibank).

## How Chinese students usually get OSHC

1. **University default / preferred provider**: added to the offer or paid with tuition.
2. **Education agent**: arranges it with the visa paperwork; some agents favour a provider.
3. **Self-purchase**: on an insurer's website.

The partner's observation (CDEV3000 kick-off): most of Medibank's Chinese student
customers come through routes 1 and 2, and many never engage with the product after
purchase. That's the gap this agent's content addresses.

## What other brands do on Chinese social media (patterns, not names)

* Generic "澳洲留学保险攻略" notes written by agents and creators, often with prices and
  comparisons. These are exactly what Medibank must not imitate.
* University accounts posting O-Week and wellbeing content: a chance for factual,
  non-endorsement references.
* Student creators sharing "第一次在澳洲看病" stories: the most trusted format.

## Medibank's differentiation to emphasise (factually, without comparison)

* 24/7 Student Health and Support Line, including counselling, legal advice and interpreter
* App with claims, provider search and Online Doctor (trial; conditions apply)
* University-specific OSHC sites and the planned student chatbot
* Student Rewards Plus (describe generally)

Say what Medibank offers. Never say others don't.
