# Personas

Five people the content is written for. Pick one per note. Names are illustrative.

---

## P1 小林 · offer holder (拿到 offer)

* 21, finishing undergrad in Chengdu, offer for a Master's at UNSW, starts in 4 months.
* Uses an education agent for the application and visa. Has never read the OSHC part of
  the offer letter: "中介说都弄好了".
* Searches 小红书 for: `UNSW 新生`, `悉尼租房`, `澳洲留学 行前清单`, `offer 之后要做什么`;
  follows the UNSW CSSA 公众号; watches 抖音 study-abroad vlogs.
* **What moves her:** a checklist she can save; a senior student saying "我当时也没看".
* **Message:** offer letter 里 OSHC 那一栏，花 3 分钟自己看懂。
* **Next step:** open her own offer letter; search 学校官网 OSHC.

## P2 阿杰 · visa stage (办签证)

* 23, accepted the offer, gathering visa documents with the agent.
* Worries about getting the visa, not about health cover.
* **What moves him:** "保单日期要覆盖整个签证期" as a concrete thing to check.
* **Message:** 保险是签证要求之一，日期和保障你自己确认一遍。
* **Risk:** never give visa advice beyond "以澳洲内政部官网为准" (AU-6).

## P3 Mia · just arrived (刚落地)

* 20, first week in Sydney. Has never seen a GP outside China; thinks you go to hospital
  for everything.
* **What moves her:** a step-by-step "第一次看 GP" with screenshots; knowing there's an
  app and a 24/7 line in Chinese-friendly terms (interpreter service).
* **Message:** 生病了先别慌，你的保险里有这些可以用。
* **Next step:** download the app, save the support line.

## P4 老周 · current student under pressure (在读)

* 25, second year of a Master's, part-time job, exam stress, sleeps badly.
* Doesn't know counselling or a nurse line is part of his cover.
* **What moves him:** a peer story, low-key tone, no lecture.
* **Message:** 撑不住的时候，有人 24 小时可以聊。
* **Risk:** mental-health wording per `crisis-playbook.md`; always include 000 / Lifeline.

## P5 林妈妈 · parent (家长)

* 50, in Hangzhou, paying for everything, worried about her daughter getting sick alone.
* Reads 公众号 articles and watches 视频号 shared in 家长群; a few use 小红书 for 留学家长 content.
* **What moves her:** reassurance with specifics: nurse line, interpreter, ambulance cover
  with its conditions.
* **Message:** 孩子在澳洲生病了，保险里有哪些支持，告诉孩子存好。
* **Next step:** share the note with her child.
