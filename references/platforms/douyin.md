# 抖音 (Douyin)

## Why it matters here

The biggest short-video platform in China, entertainment-first. Students don't come to
Douyin to research insurance, so it works for **awareness** at intake peaks through
creator vlogs ("留学第一周", "第一次在悉尼看病"), not for detailed explainers.

## Account

Enterprise account (企业号). Douyin applies extra checks to finance and insurance content
and advertisers; an overseas insurer without a China licence may not get an enterprise
account or ad access in the insurance category. Default plan: **creator content via 星图
only**, no brand account and no paid ads until Legal confirms (PL-2). [VERIFY]

## Formats

| Format | Use for | Specs |
|--------|---------|-------|
| 短视频 | Creator vlogs and "3 件事" explainers | 15–60 s, vertical 9:16, hook in 2 s, subtitles, title/description ≤ 55 chars visible |
| 图文 (photo carousel) | Checklists | 4–9 images |
| 直播 | Not recommended (hard to keep compliant at volume) | — |

Hashtags: 3–5, e.g. `#澳洲留学 #留学生日常 #悉尼生活 #留学行前准备`.

## Collaboration: 巨量星图 (Xingtu)

* Creator brand deals must be booked through 星图; deals that skip 星图 risk takedown or
  traffic limits (36氪, Jul 2018, on 星图's launch). (PL-1)
* Brief creators on the full red list; Douyin creators default to a hard-sell tone, so
  review scripts and the final cut.

## Platform-specific don'ts

* No 引流 to WeChat, phone numbers or external sites in video, captions, comments or
  profile (PL-3); Douyin enforces this strictly.
* No "保险科普" accounts that actually sell (avoid as creators: CN-1/CN-2).
* No 小黄车 / product links of any kind.
* No exaggerated "避坑" fear content ("不买这个你会倾家荡产").
