## Default channel mix

| Platform | Share of effort | Why |
|----------|-----------------|-----|
| 小红书 | 40% | Students research here before departure; search keeps notes alive for months |
| 微信公众号 + 视频号 | 30% | Owned hub; parents; durable long-form guides; links into the WeChat ecosystem |
| 抖音 | 20% | Reach at intake peaks; video walkthroughs |
| 微博 | 10% | Monitoring, topic participation, fast response; not a main content channel |

Adjust after the first month's data (`skills/cn-performance-review`).

## How the platforms connect (no off-platform links)

```
小红书 / 抖音 / 微博 note ──(words only)──► "搜索 Medibank 公众号" or "去学校官网搜 OSHC"
微信公众号 article ──► 视频号 video, menu: 学校 OSHC 指南 · 看病指南 · 联系方式 [VERIFY menu links allowed]
All roads ──► university OSHC page ──► Medibank university OSHC site + chatbot (off-platform)
```

Cross-platform mentions ("去公众号看完整版") are normal on WeChat itself. On 小红书 and
抖音, naming a WeChat account counts as diversion and is throttled (PL-3), so there
the next step is always "学校官网搜 OSHC".

## What's the same everywhere

* Paid creator content goes through the platform's own marketplace and shows its tag (PL-1).
* No prices, quotes, comparisons, purchase steps or data collection (CN-1, CN-2, CN-6).
* Ads must be identifiable as ads (CN-4, CN-5).
* Paid ads (信息流/feed ads) need finance or insurance qualifications Medibank may not
  have in China. Assume **organic + creator only** until Legal confirms (PL-2).
