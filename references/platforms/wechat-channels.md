# 微信视频号 (WeChat Channels)

## Why it matters here

Short video inside WeChat, spread through friends' likes and group-chat shares. It reaches
**parents** (P5) and WeChat-first students, and it can link to the 公众号 article with
the full guide.

## Account

## Collaboration

* **视频号互选平台**: WeChat's official marketplace where brands book Channels creators;
  creators need 10k+ followers and real-name verification to join (人人都是产品经理,
  21 Sep 2023). Paid videos go through it and carry its label (PL-1).

## Platform-specific don'ts

* In live streams: no price, no "评论区扣 1 我私你", no "点击下方购买" (CN-1, CN-2).
* No 诱导分享 or 诱导点赞 mechanics.
* Live Q&A about personal claims → move to official customer service, never discuss a
  member's case on stream (CN-6, AU-7).

## Parent-friendly angle

"孩子在澳洲生病了，保险里有哪些支持" with the 24/7 line and interpreter service, ending
"把这条转给孩子，让 TA 存好". Shares from parents to children are the goal.
