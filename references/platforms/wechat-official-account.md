# 微信公众号 (WeChat Official Account)

## Why it matters here

WeChat is where students and their parents already are, and where universities,
student unions (CSSA) and education agents publish. A 公众号 is Medibank's **owned hub**:
long, searchable guides that every other platform can point to by name.

## Formats

| Format | Use for | Specs |
|--------|---------|-------|
| 长图文 article | Definitive guides: 《offer letter 里 OSHC 怎么看》《第一次在澳洲看 GP》 | 1,200–2,500 chars; H2 sub-heads allowed; cover 2.35:1; summary ≤ 120 chars; title ≤ 30 chars |
| 图片消息 / 贴图 | Checklists | 3–9 images |
| 合集 (tag collections) | Series | Group guides by journey stage |

Unlike 小红书, sub-headings and structure are **expected** here; the AI-flavour checks still
apply to the prose.

## Collaboration

* **互选平台** (WeChat's official marketplace for 公众号 and 视频号 placements) for paid
  articles on student or university-life accounts (PL-1). Placements carry an ad label.
* Co-publishing with university international offices or student unions: factual,
  non-endorsement, and only with a written agreement.

## Platform-specific don'ts

* No mini-program or link that lets users buy or get a quote (CN-1, CN-2).
* No 留言 prompts asking people to post passport, CoE or phone numbers (CN-6).
* No 诱导分享 ("转发到 3 个群领资料"): WeChat penalises induced sharing. [VERIFY current rule text]

## What other platforms can say

On WeChat itself (视频号, 朋友圈 shares, other 公众号): "关注 Medibank 公众号，菜单里有完整指南" is fine.
On 小红书 and 抖音: don't name the 公众号 (PL-3).
