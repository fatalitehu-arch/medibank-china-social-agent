# 微博 (Weibo)

## Why it matters here

Public, fast and topic-driven. Universities, alumni groups and news outlets post here, and
complaints spread here first. For Medibank it's mainly a **listening and response**
channel, plus topic participation at intake peaks (e.g. #澳洲留学# during O-Week).

## Listening (the main job)

Track weekly: `Medibank`, `OSHC`, `澳洲 留学 保险`, `留学生 看病 澳洲`, university names +
`保险`. Log anything negative using the levels in `references/crisis-playbook.md`.
Weibo is where L2 issues become L3: escalate early.

## Platform-specific don'ts

* Don't join trending topics unrelated to students' health (topic-jacking reads as spam
  and risks political topics).
* Never argue with critics in public replies; follow the crisis playbook.
* Links to external sites are technically possible but still no purchase or quote pages
  (CN-1, CN-2).
