## Why it matters here

Students **search** 小红书 before they leave: `OSHC`, `澳洲看病`, `UNSW 新生`,
`留学行前清单`. Search keeps a good explainer alive for months, so this is the main
platform for the 看懂自己的保险 message.

## Formats

| Format | Use for | Specs |
|--------|---------|-------|
| 图文 | Checklists, explainers, how-to | 6–9 images (3:4), cover with large text; body 300–800 chars; title ≤ 20 chars |
| 视频 | First GP visit, app walkthrough, vlog | 30–90 s, vertical, subtitles, hook in 3 s |
| 合集 | Series | Group 《看懂你的保险》 notes so search visitors find the rest |

Hashtags: 5–10 per note. Pool: `#澳洲留学 #留学生活 #澳洲生活 #悉尼留学 #墨尔本留学 #OSHC #留学行前准备 #澳洲看病 #留学生保险 #新生指南`.

## Distribution

* **发现页 (feed)**: a new note goes to a small pool, then bigger pools if it engages.
  Cover and hook decide the second round.
* **搜索 (search)**: ranks on title, body, tags and image text. Put the core keyword in the
  first 10 characters of the title and once in the first paragraph.
* Commonly reported signal weights: follows > comments/shares > likes/saves. Saves still
  matter most for us (they mean "useful").
