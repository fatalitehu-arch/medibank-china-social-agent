# Student strategy: "看懂自己的保险" (Know your own cover)

## The problem (from the Medibank partner brief, CDEV3000 MB Group 6)

* Medibank's OSHC customer numbers among international students are falling.
* The product's features are good, but students don't know they exist.
* Students let their education/visa agent arrange OSHC and never look at the policy
  themselves, so they don't know what they're covered for, how to claim, or that the
  24/7 support line and app exist.

## The campaign idea

**Core message:** 保险是你自己的。中介可以帮你办，但保了什么、怎么用，你自己要看懂。
("Your agent can arrange it, but it's your cover. Know what's in it.")

**Purpose:** build a direct relationship between Medibank and Chinese students on Chinese
social media *before* the education agent decides everything for them.

Goal: students know Medibank, realise student insurance matters, and want to learn about
it themselves. A later chatbot on the Medibank university OSHC site handles detailed
policy questions.

So social content should spark the thought "insurance actually matters, I should look
into it", not explain every policy detail. When a post or comment needs detail, point in
words to the university OSHC page and its chatbot. We are not asking students to buy
anything on social media.

### The funnel

```
Social post: 小红书 · 抖音 · 微博 · 视频号 (brand or creator)
   │  or 公众号 guide (owned hub)
   │  "打开你的 offer letter，找到 OSHC 那一栏"
   ▼
Student checks their own offer letter / CoE
   │  sees the OSHC arrangement and the university's OSHC page
   ▼
University OSHC page (e.g. UNSW: preferred provider is Medibank)
   │  links to the university's Medibank OSHC site (UNSW: medibankoshc.com.au/unsw/)
   ▼
Medibank university OSHC site + the student chatbot (team's redesign)
   │  student asks questions in their own language, activates cover, downloads app
   ▼
Medibank app: claims, Online Doctor, 24/7 Student Health and Support Line
```

Public posts name the destination in words ("去你学校官网搜 OSHC" /
"Medibank 官网") and contain **no QR code, phone number or purchase link** (PL-3).
Inside WeChat, 视频号 videos can point to the 公众号 guide. Platform-by-platform roles:
`references/platforms/overview.md`.

### Message rules for the "don't just rely on your agent" angle

* ✅ Empowering: "中介帮你办好了，也花 3 分钟自己看一眼"
* ✅ Practical: "offer letter 里 OSHC 那一栏怎么看", "保单生效日期要覆盖整个签证期"
* ❌ Attacking agents: "中介都在坑你", "中介拿回扣". Education agents are a real and
  legitimate channel for Medibank and universities; disparaging them is a commercial
  and ACL (s 18) risk.
* ❌ Telling students to switch insurer or cancel a policy. That is advice about a
  specific product decision (CN-2 consulting) and can leave a student uncovered.
* ❌ Visa advice beyond "OSHC is a visa condition, check Home Affairs" (AU-6).

## Audience segments and what each needs

| Segment | When | What they worry about | Content that works | Next step we point to |
|---------|------|-----------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| 拿到 offer (offer holder) | 3–6 months before start | "OSHC 是什么，要不要我自己买" | Offer letter 解读, OSHC vs Medicare | Read the OSHC part of your offer letter |
| 办签证 (visa stage) | 2–3 months before | "保单日期对不对", certificate | Checklist: dates must cover the whole visa | University OSHC page |
| 准备出发 (pre-departure) | 0–1 month before | packing, arrival | 行前清单 with "激活保险 + 下载 app" | Activate cover, download app |
| 刚落地 (first 8 weeks) | O-Week to Week 6 | first time sick, finding a GP | "第一次在悉尼看病" walkthrough | App: find a provider, Online Doctor |
| 在读 (current student) | Semester | stress, exams, after-hours illness | Mental health support, 半夜不舒服怎么办 | 24/7 Student Health and Support Line |
| 家长 (parents) | Any | "孩子生病了谁管" | Explain support line and interpreter service | Share with your child |

| Window | Focus |

## Why creators (KOC/KOL) carry most of the message

This applies on 小红书, 抖音, 视频号 and 微博. On WeChat, Medibank's own 公众号 is the hub.

1. Students trust peers who have lived it more than a brand (a Medibank account will
   read as an ad, even when it's helpful).
2. "Check your own cover" works best as a first-person story: "我当时完全没看过自己的保单…"
3. Creators already rank for the searches students make on 小红书 and 抖音: 澳洲留学
   行前清单, 悉尼看病, OSHC 是什么, UNSW 新生.

Default mix: **70% creator content via each platform's marketplace, 30% brand-owned**
(the 公众号 hub plus explainers the creators can point back to). Channel split is in
`references/platforms/overview.md` (小红书 40 · WeChat 30 · 抖音 20 · 微博 10). Adjust after
the first month's data.

## What to measure

| Stage | Metric | Where |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Reach | Impressions, reads, video views | Each platform's dashboard and marketplace (蒲公英, 星图, 互选, 微任务) |
| Engagement | Saves/收藏, shares (esp. WeChat), comments asking real questions | All platforms |
| Intent | Search volume for "OSHC", "Medibank"; 公众号 follows; menu clicks | 小红书/抖音 search, 微信指数, 公众号 dashboard [VERIFY tools] |
| Action | Visits to the university Medibank OSHC site and chatbot sessions from China | Medibank web analytics (UTM not possible in-note; use a campaign-specific landing phrase or chatbot greeting question "你从哪里知道我们的？") |
| Outcome | App downloads/activations among new students; support-line usage | Medibank internal |

Never collect personal data on social platforms to measure (CN-6).
