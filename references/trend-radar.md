## 令人心动的offer 第八季 · use now

* **Why it fits:** students and parents are watching medical life up close; "what happens
  when I go to hospital" is exactly our 在澳洲看病 pillar. The director's line that almost
  everyone ends up in orthopaedics at some point is a natural hook.
* **Angle:** "人一辈子总要进一次骨科。那在澳洲呢？" → what happens at an Australian
  emergency department, when you see a GP first, and why to check your own OSHC before you
  need it.
* **Format & platform:** 小红书 图文 explainer (owned); 抖音 / 小红书 creator vlog by an
  Australian medical or nursing student KOC; 微博 topic post on episode nights.
* **Watch out (TR-1, TR-2):** name the show as a topic only; no clips, stills, logo, cast
  names or Tencent Video branding.
* **Shelf life:** while the season airs.
* **Sources:** 新京报 via 腾讯新闻, 2026-10-21, https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20261021A05E0N00

## 闪身步 · adapt (late)

* **Why it fits:** a playful trend with a built-in health angle: injuries from copying it
  made the news, which is a natural way into "what to do if you get hurt in Australia".
* **Angle:** "跳闪身步扭到了？在澳洲先别硬扛" → when to rest, when to see a GP, when it's an
  emergency (000), and that the Student Health and Support Line is there 24/7.
  Physiotherapy and imaging cover depends on the policy: say "以保单条款为准" and confirm
  with `references/brand-facts.md` before naming any benefit.
* **Format & platform:** 抖音 / 小红书 creator short video (a student doing the step
  safely, then the tip); no owned "everyone try it" post.
* **Watch out (TR-2, TR-5):** don't encourage copying; creators use their own footage and
  platform-licensed music.
* **Shelf life:** peaked in October; post by mid-November or skip.
* **Sources:** 腾讯新闻, 2026-09-24, https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260924A08TD400 ·
  上海金山 official account via 澎湃, 2026-11-02 (doctor's injury advice),
  https://thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34385072

## 后会友期 · adapt (format only)

* **Why it fits:** friendship and reunions resonate with students leaving home; the
  show's format ("checking in on old friends") can carry a light 身心健康 or
  看懂你的保险 message.
* **Angle:** "出国前和好朋友互相检查一件事：保险你自己看过吗？" or a 留学搭子 series on
  looking after each other abroad.
* **Format & platform:** 小红书 creator 图文 with two friends; 公众号 piece on loneliness
  and support in the first semester.
* **Watch out (TR-1, TR-3, TR-6):** the show's main storyline is a real feud between two
  guests, so never reference the guests or take sides; the show has an insurer among its
  sponsors (中国平安), so no reference to sponsors and creator-led only.
* **Shelf life:** while the season airs.
* **Sources:** 新浪新闻, Oct–Nov 2026, https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7879776328_1d5abd84806802gfda.html
