# Content playbook (brand account + creator notes)

## Content pillars

Every note belongs to one pillar. Target mix per month for the brand account:

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 看懂你的保险 (Know your cover) | 35% | offer letter 里 OSHC 那一栏怎么看; 保单日期要覆盖整个签证; 中介帮你办了，你也要知道这 5 件事 |
| 2 在澳洲看病 (Using healthcare) | 30% | 第一次在悉尼看 GP; GP、急诊、000 的区别; 半夜发烧怎么办 |
| 3 用好你的 app 和热线 (Use the services) | 20% | 3 步在 app 里报销; 24 小时热线能帮你什么（护士、心理、翻译、法律） |
| 4 留学生活与身心健康 (Wellbeing) | 15% | 开学焦虑; 考试周睡不好; 冬天流感季准备 |

## Brand voice

Persona: **一位在澳洲读过书的学姐**, working for Medibank, who has seen many new
students get stuck. Speaks plainly, warmly, with concrete detail.

* Talk to one person: 你, not 各位同学们.
* Start from a scene, not a definition: "落地第三天嗓子疼，打开地图发现附近的诊所都要预约…"
* Concrete beats general: name the step, the screen, the document.
* Honest about limits: if something has conditions, say them.
* Light emoji as section markers (📄 🩺 📱), max one per paragraph. No emoji in titles.
* No 广告腔: 赶快、立即、超值、限时、强烈推荐.
* The brand account says it is Medibank. It never pretends to be a random student.

## Note templates

### 图文 explainer (most common)

```
[Hook: one concrete scene or question, 1–2 lines]
[Why it matters to you, 1–2 lines]
📄 第一步 …
📄 第二步 …
📄 第三步 …
[One honest caveat / 以官网条款为准]
[Soft next step: 去学校官网搜 OSHC / 打开 app 看看]
[Interaction question: 你当时是自己办的还是中介办的？]
```

Images (6–9): cover with the cover line → one step per image with an annotated
mock-up (blur personal data) → summary card. Use mock-ups of the offer letter and the
app, never a real student's document.

### 短视频 script (30–60 s)

```
0–3 s   Hook on screen + voice: "你知道你的 OSHC 什么时候到期吗？"
3–20 s  Show the 3 things to check (screen recording / mock offer letter)
20–40 s One real scene (creator) or service explainer (brand)
40–55 s Where to look next, in words
55–60 s Question for comments
```
